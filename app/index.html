        How to use this guide

        • Checklist: tick a trophy off when you earn it. It stays ticked in this browser.
        • Guide: click any trophy to open its guide — what to do, where and how, when it can be missed, and the sources each fact comes from.
        • Filters: filter by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online), by grade, by earned or still to earn, or search by name or mission.
        • Hide story spoilers: with the spoiler setting on, story trophies show as “Hidden story trophy” until you earn them.
        • Export / Import: export your progress to a file, and import it back on another browser or after clearing data.
        • Roadmap: six stages in the order to earn them. Missables: the thirteen to watch while you play.

        Where the facts come from

        Names, descriptions and grades are PlayStation’s official list, as carried by Exophase and PSNProfiles, who agree on every name, description and grade; the same 55 appear on TrueAchievements. Counts PSN tracks (42 Shooting Range challenges, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas and the rest) come from PSNProfiles. The missable warning and the no-mission-replay note come from KeenGamer, GTA Zone (PowerPyx) and Output Lag. Every trophy’s guide names its sources beside it. Where a step follows only from the official description, the guide says so — and where the description gives no step at all, it says that too, rather than inventing one.

        What this guide does not do

        • It does not claim a specific location or step without a cited source.
        • It does not use Rockstar’s logo, official artwork, box art or screenshots of the game — the look here is our own.
        • It does not fetch anything: it runs entirely from its own files, with the network off.
        • It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Sony or PlayStation. Grand Theft Auto and GTA are trademarks of Take-Two Interactive.
